# Issue draft 06 — prefill chunking at 6144 instead of 2048: 2.67× less redundant reconstruct, +13–15 % measured on the linears alone

**Repo:** local-inference-lab/vllm (docs/serving guidance; no code) · **Cites:** docs/47 F5.1, F8, plan P1.2

## Summary
Because dequantized weights are rebuilt per matrix per chunk (draft 05), a 32k prompt at 2048-token
chunks decodes all 24.33 B weights 16×; at 6144, 6×. Phase-timed on real weights
(receipts/kernel-gap-prefill-phases.json): effective linear throughput rises from 289.8 to 331.4
TFLOPS on the 5120×17408 gate matrix (+13–15 %) before counting the 2.67× fewer chunk passes and
per-chunk fixed overheads. Estimated +15–25 % end-to-end prefill.

## MEASURED (2026-08-17, full requalification ladder, receipts/kernel-gap-chunk6144-requal.json)
+3.3 % PP at 32k, **+8.4 % at 197k, +9.3 % at 259k** (−10.3 s wall on a 259k request); decode
unchanged (+2.0 %, between-boot variance); fit −1.6 % KV tokens; **needle exact at 197k ×3 depths
and at 258,962 tokens = 98.8 % of the 262,144 window, engine alive afterwards, on BOTH arms.**
The +15–25 % estimate was 2–4× high at short context (per-matrix linear gains dilute through the
~40 % of chunk time the linears do not own); the long-context number approaches it. APC was off in
this profile — with APC+align the 1600-token clipping (docs/47 F5.5) would cap the effective chunk
and this A/B must be re-run.

## Caveat that makes this a qualification, not a flag flip
`--max-num-batched-tokens` interacts with KV budget, CUDA-graph rows, MTP token slots, and
long-context stability. The validation ladder is mandatory, in particular the FULL-window needle:
a prior config passed 4 of 5 gates and then died with EngineDeadError on the first 262k request
(docs/46 §21). Ladder: fit at published util → PP A/B → full-window needle → C1 decode unchanged →
concurrency knee re-check.

## What would falsify this
The knee re-check: if 6144-token chunks steal enough decode slots at C4–C8 to regress aggregate
throughput, the setting qualifies only as a prefill-heavy profile variant, not a default.
