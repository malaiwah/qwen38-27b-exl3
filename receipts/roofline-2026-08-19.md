# How close is this to the hardware's limits? Measured roofline for the RTX 5090

> **SUPERSEDED IN PART — read `receipts/power-limit-2026-08-19.md` first.**
> Every measurement in this receipt was taken while the card was capped at **400 W of
> its 600 W default** (by `lactd` *and* by the systemd unit), and with a benchmark too
> short to raise clocks off idle. The corrected figures are: fp16 **214.5** (not 199),
> bf16 **217.6** (not 203), fp8 **630.7** (not 559.4); bandwidth is unchanged at
> 1840.5 GB/s because it was never power-limited. Flagship prefill is **9,638.9**
> (not 7,694.9). The *conclusion* below is unchanged and in fact stronger: the
> fast/faithful gap widened from 3.9x to 4.9x. Corrected harness:
> `tools/roofline-sustained.py`.

**Date:** 2026-08-19
**Method:** every number below is measured on this card — HBM bandwidth and dense
GEMM rates by microbenchmark (`tools/roofline.py`), per-format in-model rates from
the production bake-off on real checkpoint shapes at M=2051, and served numbers
from the n=3 Phase-0 harness. Nothing is taken from a spec sheet.

## The card, measured

| quantity | measured |
|---|---|
| achievable HBM bandwidth | **1842.6 GB/s** (1.84 TB/s), flat from 256 MiB to 2 GiB |
| dense fp16 GEMM | 199 TFLOP/s |
| dense bf16 GEMM | 203 TFLOP/s |
| dense fp8 GEMM (`_scaled_mm`) | **559.4 TFLOP/s** |
| implied fp4 silicon peak (2× fp8) | ~1119 TFLOP/s |
| SMs / VRAM | 170 / 31.40 GiB |

## The model, counted from `config.json`

H=5120, L=64 (16 full-attention + 48 linear-attention), I=17408, V=248320.
Counted matmul parameters: MLP 17.11e9 + full-attn 1.17e9 + lm_head 1.27e9 =
**19.56e9**, i.e. **39.1 GFLOP per token** (2·params; excludes GDN projections and
norms, so prefill ceilings below are mildly optimistic).

## In-model GEMM rates, by weight format

| format | effective rate | source |
|---|---|---|
| FP4 (NVFP4, b12x) | **486.9 TFLOP/s** | bake-off 164.7 ms/chunk, real shapes |
| FP6 (MXFP6, b12x) | 282.5 TFLOP/s | bake-off 283.9 ms/chunk |
| trellis (b12x W4A16) | 96.1 TFLOP/s | measured all-b12x PP ceiling 2457.7 |

**FP4 kernel efficiency is 43.5% of silicon peak** (486.9 of ~1119). The missing
half is the activation quantise step plus the fact that these are real
non-square shapes, not an 8192³ microbenchmark.

## Prefill: where each profile sits against *its own format's* ceiling

| profile | measured PP | format ceiling | efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| `throughput` (all-FP4) | 7694.9 ± 21.8 | 12,453 | **61.8%** |
| `balanced` (gate_up FP6) | 3266.3 ± 13.9 | 7,224 | 45.2% |
| `fidelity` (all-trellis) | 1965.4 ± 2.1 | 2,458 | **80.0%** |

Stacking the two factors for the flagship: 61.8% of the kernel ceiling × 43.5%
kernel efficiency = **26.9% of raw silicon FP4 peak**. The 38.2% lost above the
kernel is non-GEMM work — attention, the 48 GDN layers, norms, sampling — which
the nsys trace put at ~101 ms of a 266 ms chunk.

`fidelity` at **80.0%** is the most saturated of the three: the trellis path has
very little left to give, which matches this session's experience (four
consecutive optimisations moved it 1080.6 → 1965.4 and the last one bought 0.1%).

## Decode: 26-40% of the weight-bandwidth roofline

A decode step must stream every weight once, so the ceiling is
`bandwidth / weight_bytes` target forwards/s. With MTP=6, accepted tokens per step
is `1 + 6·acceptance`.

| run | measured | steps/s | ceiling | efficiency | theoretical max TG |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `throughput` fox | 185.0 [acc 0.930] | 28.1 | 108.1 | 26.0% | 711 |
| `throughput` essay | 93.3 [acc 0.298] | 33.5 | 108.1 | 31.0% | 301 |
| `fidelity` fox | 207.7 [acc 1.000] | 29.7 | 91.1 | 32.6% | 638 |
| `fidelity` essay | 93.1 [acc 0.304] | 33.0 | 91.1 | 36.2% | 257 |
| `balanced` essay | 95.7 [acc 0.324] | 32.5 | 80.9 | **40.2%** | 238 |

Every profile lands at **28-34 steps/s regardless of weight size** — the tell that
decode is **not** bandwidth-bound but latency/overhead-bound, consistent with the
nsys finding. In principle there is 2.5-3.8× of headroom here; in practice it sits
behind per-step launch and Python cost, and the MTP draft loop's 6 sequential
sub-steps, not behind memory traffic.

## The criterion-1 wall, in one line each

Criterion 1 asks for 7000 tok/s prefill *while* criterion 3 asks for KLD ≤ 0.012.
Only FP6 and trellis satisfy the fidelity side, and:

| format | prefill ceiling | efficiency needed to hit 7000 |
|---|---|---|
| FP4 | 12,453 | 56.2% — **achieved (61.8%)**, but KLD 0.063759 fails |
| **FP6** | **7,224** | **96.9%** — no implementation on this stack exceeds 80% |
| trellis | 2,458 | 284.8% — impossible |

**That is the whole answer to why 6/6 is unreachable.** The only KLD-passing
format has a GEMM-bound ceiling of 7,224 tok/s, so criterion 1 would require
96.9% of theoretical peak *after* the format is chosen — where the best efficiency
this stack has ever achieved on any format is 80.0%, and 61.8% on the FP4 path
that actually has headroom.

Resident FP8 would clear it on speed (ceiling 14,307) but not on memory:

```
22.6 (FP8 weights) + 8.89 (KV @238,400) + 1.96 (activation) + 0.52 (graphs)
  + 0.30 (non-torch) = 34.27 GiB  vs  31.40 available  ->  short 2.87 GiB
```

At best it fits 6.02 GiB of KV = **~161,400 tokens, 68% of the context criterion**.
So the two criteria are separated by a genuine hardware boundary — 2.87 GiB of
VRAM, or about one more memory stack — not by missing engineering.

## Honest caveats

- 39.1 GFLOP/token excludes GDN projections and norms, so the prefill ceilings are
  optimistic and the efficiencies quoted are therefore slightly pessimistic.
- The fp4 silicon peak is inferred as 2× the measured fp8 rate rather than measured
  directly; no dense fp4 `_scaled_mm` path was available to benchmark.
- `copy_` bandwidth (1842.6 GB/s) exceeds the 1792 GB/s nameplate, so some L2
  benefit is likely included; using the nameplate instead would *raise* the decode
  efficiencies by ~3%.
