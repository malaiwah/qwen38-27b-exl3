# DSpark vs built-in MTP on the EXL3 target: measured, and MTP stays

**Date:** 2026-08-19 (late). Fork issue filed: local-inference-lab/vllm#442.

## Getting it to boot took three fixes, each a finding

1. **Architecture misroute (fork bug, #442):** `RadixArk/Qwen3.8-27B-DSpark` ships
   `architectures: ["DSparkDraftModel"]` + `model_type: "qwen3"`; the fork's dspark
   normalization checks architectures only and rewrites the draft to `deepseek_v4`.
   One-line config fix (-> `Qwen3DSparkModel`) passes.
2. **Repo-ID resolution:** the launcher mounts the host HF hub at
   `/root/.cache/huggingface` without a `hub/` level, so repo-ID lookups fail inside
   the container; the draft must be passed as the in-container snapshot path.
3. **fp8-KV incompatibility (fork limitation, #442):** the pure-attention draft
   selects FLASH_ATTN independently of the hybrid target's TRITON_ATTN, and
   FLASH_ATTN rejects `fp8_e4m3` KV -> external drafts are unusable on fp8-KV
   deployments. A/B ran at `auto` KV (2x KV cost), 50k ctx.
   Also: the draft inherits the target's `--quantization exl3` unless the
   speculative config overrides it (`"quantization": null`) - exactly the trap
   docs/56 SS3.3 warned about.

## The A/B (throughput profile, 50k ctx, auto KV, single stream)

| | PP | fox | essay | draft cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DSpark (BF16, block 7) | 8,976.6 | **183.0** [acc 0.857] | 74.8 [acc 0.147] | 2.7 GB + KV |
| MTP-6 (built-in) | **9,495.9** | 175.0 [acc 0.944] | **76.7** [acc 0.221] | 0 |

**Verdict: MTP stays.** DSpark's single-pass block drafting wins +4.6% on repetitive
decode despite LOWER acceptance (drafting cost, not draft quality), loses essay
-2.5% because the FP8-trained draft's acceptance halves against our EXL3 target on
free-form text, and pays 5.5% PP plus 2.7 GB VRAM the context budget cannot spare.

This also settles the satgeze question on our stack: their claim was "target
quantization quality decides low-bit draft acceptance". Our target is HIGHER
fidelity than any quant they tested, yet the FP8-trained draft still accepts only
0.147 on essays vs the co-trained MTP head's 0.221 - so *train/serve output-
distribution match* matters alongside target quality. An EXL3-target-trained DSpark
(docs/56 SS7.4, "would be a first") remains the open question; measured decision
input for the user: the ceiling it must beat is now known precisely.

## DFlash2 port assessment (docs/56 SS7.3), closed with local data

DFlash2 would bring block-parallel drafting WITH higher acceptance than MTP (their
5.46 vs 5.02 GSM8K), i.e. the fox win without the essay loss. But: 3 missing fork
components (selector, two-tap conv, bidirectional mask), no training code published,
+3.8 GB BF16 draft (or ~1.3 GB re-quantized), and our decode criterion is already
met (228.3/104.1 on fidelity). The port is real engineering for a criterion we
pass; the requant path attacks the criterion we fail. **Not now; revisit if decode
becomes the binding constraint.**
