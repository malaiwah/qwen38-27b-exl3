## 2. reconstruct→hgemm is still worth keeping (+6.2%)

Open question after the cure: with `ANY_BITS=1` sending K5 to B12X, is the
reconstruct route dead code? **No.**

| | PP |
|---|---|
| `RECONSTRUCT_M=1` (default) | **2970.1** |
| `RECONSTRUCT_M=0` | 2797.5 |

+6.2% for keeping it. Whichever matrices B12X still declines (outside the
`N_RANGE` window, non-MCG, or not 128-divisible) are worth routing through
reconstruct rather than the fused kernel. The fold-48 budget and the shared arena
therefore remain live and relevant, not vestigial.

## 3. The PREFILL_CAPACITY inversion did NOT replicate — and the reason is instructive

Earlier, n=1: capacity 3072 → 1965.9, 2048 → 2404.0, 1024 → 2416.2 (**+22%** for
*lowering* it). At n=3 with the cure live:

| capacity | PP (n=3) | TG-fox |
|---|---|---|
| 3072 | 2979.4 ± 8.4 | 229.1 ± 0.9 |
| 1024 | 2998.3 ± 15.7 | 229.0 ± 0.0 |

**+0.6% — inside noise.** The dial's effect was never about the arena; it was about
*which kernel carried the large matrices*. When K5 prefill went through
reconstruct→hgemm, capacity mattered enormously; once K5 moved to B12X, it stopped
mattering at all. Both numbers are real for their configuration.

This is why the correction was gated on n=3 before being published: the inverted
claim was posted to nobody. The transferable version — *the capacity dial's effect
is dominated by the prefill kernel in play, not by the arena* — has now been posted
to b12x#235 and rtx6kpro#79, with the n=1-then-n=3 provenance stated plainly.
