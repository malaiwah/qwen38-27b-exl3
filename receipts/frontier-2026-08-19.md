fixes + mnbt 3072 + util 0.93 + int6 embeds), not inherited from earlier

an irreducible tradeoff be surfaced **with measurements** rather than silently

## The proof that no configuration satisfies all six

Three independent measured facts close the box:

1. **KLD <= 0.012 requires FP6-or-better essentially everywhere.**
   From the validated additive model (`receipts/kld-axis-conclusion-2026-08-19.md`,
   held-out prediction accurate to 1.1%): the budget after the irreducible floor
   is `0.012 - 0.00468 = 0.00732`, and the *smallest single FP4 group*
   (`down_proj`, 0.0127) already exceeds it. No FP4 subset assignment fits.
2. **FP6 everywhere costs 5.03 GiB of weights** (20.91 vs 15.88 GiB), which
   leaves 4.35 GiB of KV and a hard ceiling of **99,552 tokens** — measured, the
   engine refuses 238,400 with "estimated maximum model length is 99552".
   Therefore **KLD <= 0.012 provably forces context to ~40% of the criterion.**
3. **FP6 everywhere also costs PP**: 4648 vs 7666 (-39%), because prefill is
   **92% GPU-utilised** (`receipts/nsys-utilization-2026-08-19.md`) so FP6's
   1.8-2.1x GEMM cost passes straight to wall-clock with no overhead slack to
   absorb it.

Partial mixes do not escape. Working the additive model:

| trellis on | FP4 on | predicted KLD | context |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | all | 0.0568 (meas. 0.0638) | 250,000 |
| gate_up | rest | 0.0396 | ~200k |
| gate_up + GDN | rest | 0.0244 | ~180k |
| gate_up + GDN + down | attn only | **0.0117** (passes!) | ~150-190k, and MLP-on-trellis is the slow prefill path (b12x route measured 18.83 GiB weights -> 158k ctx and died on a 2051-token prefill) |

So the one mix that satisfies KLD also violates context and PP.

## What would break the deadlock

A format that is **FP4-sized in bytes and FP8-accurate in activations** —
i.e. W4A8. It would keep all-FP4's 15.88 GiB footprint (hence 250k context and
FP4-class GEMM cost) while cutting the dominant error term, since activation
quantisation is what FP4 loses on. Filed as **b12x issue #232**; our probe
confirmed it does not exist today: `dense_gemm` with `a_fmt=e4m3, b_fmt=e2m1`
fails `packed_k_bytes must be divisible by 3` because e2m1 routes into the
MXFP6 3:4 packing path.

## Recommendation, pending the human's call

Keep **all-FP4 as the default** (4/6, and the only profile meeting PP, context
and TG-essay simultaneously). all-FP6 is available behind one env var for
fidelity-first work at 99k context and is recorded here as the known path back.

The two axes that remain unmet and their exact distance:
- **TG fox: 184.8 +/- 1.0 vs 190** — 2.7% short, and ~5 sigma, so it is a real
  gap rather than noise. all-FP6 reaches 189.0 but at 99k context.
- **KLD 0.0638 vs 0.012 and p99 0.701 vs 0.15** — blocked on W4A8 per above.
