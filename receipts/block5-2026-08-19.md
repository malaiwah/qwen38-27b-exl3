## 2. The MTP depth dial is now closed at BOTH ends

Hypothesis from the spec-decode research doc: essay acceptance 0.324 means deep drafts
are mostly rejected, so shallower depth might raise essay TG. **Refuted:**

| depth | essay | essay acc | fox | fox acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 99.9 | 0.470 | 153.2 | 1.000 |
| 4 | 92.7 | 0.335 | 176.4 | 1.000 |
| **6 (default)** | **104.1** | 0.324 | **228.3** | 1.000 |

Acceptance *rate* rises at shallow depth (0.470 at d3) but tokens-per-accepted-pass
falls faster; fox scales almost linearly with depth at acc 1.000 (pure (1+d) arithmetic).
With the earlier 4/6/8/10 flat result on the throughput profile, depth 6 is optimal on
every measured workload; the dial is closed.
