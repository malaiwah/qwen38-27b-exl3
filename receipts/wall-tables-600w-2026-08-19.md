**Date:** 2026-08-19 (late)
**Why:** the bake-off, the 43.5 % kernel-efficiency figure, the "26.9 % of silicon"
headline and the criterion-1 wall table were all derived under the 400 W cap
(peer-review item). Re-measured: `receipts/traces/bakeoff-600w-2026-08-19.txt`
(`tools/kernel-bakeoff.py 1,2051`, same shapes, same tool, 600 W).

Notes. (1) The tool has never had an FP6 column; the 283.9 ms FP6 figure came from the
quant-timing matrix. FP6's 600 W ceiling is therefore **bounded from production**: all-FP6
PP moved 4,648 → 4,742 (+2.0 %), so its ceiling is ~7,224–7,370. (2) The fused trellis
kernel gains +22 % at kernel level, yet the *fidelity profile* measured +0.0 % — its
prefill is b12x + hgemm dominated, and the all-b12x production bound (2,457.7) was
measured on a path shown power-insensitive, so it stands.

## Efficiency chain for the flagship, corrected

| quantity | 400 W (stale) | 600 W (current) |
|---|---|---|
| in-model FP4 GEMM rate | 487 TFLOP/s | **557 TFLOP/s** |
| FP4 silicon peak (2× measured fp8) | 1,119 | **1,261** |
| kernel efficiency vs silicon | 43.5 % | **44.1 %** |
| measured PP / format ceiling | 7,694.9 / 12,453 = 61.8 % | **9,638.9 / 14,243 = 67.7 %** |
| PP vs raw silicon | 26.9 % | **29.9 %** |

Kernel efficiency is power-invariant (43.5 → 44.1 %) — the cap scaled everything down
together, as it should. End-to-end efficiency *rose* (61.8 → 67.7 %) because the
non-GEMM remainder (attention, GDN, norms) gained more from clocks than the GEMMs did.
